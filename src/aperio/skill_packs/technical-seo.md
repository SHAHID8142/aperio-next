Review crawlability: robots directives, crawlable links, and indexable page states. Define a canonical strategy per page type and apply it consistently. Add structured data only where the content genuinely supports it. Check sitemap accuracy, indexing readiness, and mobile usability as observed issues, not assumptions. Never promise rankings, traffic, or timelines; search outcomes are not under your control. Any measured data requires real Search Console or analytics access; do not estimate numbers and present them as measured.
