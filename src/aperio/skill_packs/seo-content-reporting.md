Review content against search intent: does each page answer the query it targets, with claims cited to credible sources. Judge citation quality, not citation count; primary and current sources outrank repeated secondary ones. In client reporting, label every figure's provenance explicitly: measured (from verified analytics access), estimated (method stated), or unknown. Do not blend the categories or present estimates as measurements. Flag content gaps and overlapping targeting as observations for technical SEO review, not as commitments.
