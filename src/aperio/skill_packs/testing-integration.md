Plan tests in layers: functional paths from the reviewed requirements, integration seams between components, accessibility checks on real flows, and performance against representative data volumes. Record evidence for every run (what executed, against which environment, and the observed result), not just pass or fail summaries. Note limitations honestly: untested browsers, mocked services, sampled performance runs. Do not present a plan as executed results, and do not extrapolate one environment's outcome to every environment.
