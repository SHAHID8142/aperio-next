Extract, from the client brief: goals, audience, in-scope items, explicit exclusions, and open unknowns. Restate the scope as one list the client can correct. Flag every ambiguity as a question; do not resolve ambiguity by assuming a commitment. Record exclusions verbatim so later work cannot quietly expand them. Do not invent deadlines, deliverables, or promises the brief does not state. If requirements conflict, surface the conflict instead of choosing silently.
